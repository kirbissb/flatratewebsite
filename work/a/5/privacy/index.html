This page explains what Holloway Electric learns about you through this website and what we do with it

What we collect

When you send the request form, we get what you type in it: your name, phone, street address or ZIP and what is going on

When you call, text or email us, we get your phone number or email address and what you tell us

This website does not use cookies, ads or tracking tools. The company that hosts it may record basic technical details of each visit, such as your IP address and browser, to keep the site running and safe

How we use it

We use your details to answer your request, to arrange and do the work, and to send you prices and bills. Requests you send through the form reach us through Web3Forms

Companies that handle your details for us: the company that hosts this website and Web3Forms, which delivers the form to us

We do not sell your information or give it to other companies to advertise to you

If you text us, we do not share your mobile number or your consent to get texts with anyone for marketing. Reply STOP and we stop texting you

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us below
